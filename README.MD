📦 Inventory & Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing inventory performance and supply chain efficiency using key operational metrics and interactive visualizations.
The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders quickly understand inventory health, warehouse capacity usage, transportation costs, and order fulfillment challenges across categories and regions.

The goal of this project is to demonstrate practical data analytics skills applied to real-world supply chain problems, with an emphasis on decision-support insights rather than just visual aesthetics.

🎯 Key Objectives

Monitor inventory efficiency and movement

Identify warehouse utilization patterns

Analyze transportation cost distribution

Track lead times and backorders

Provide category- and region-level inventory visibility

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following critical supply chain KPIs:

Warehouse Utilization Rate
Measures how effectively warehouse capacity is being used.

Days Sales of Inventory (DSI)
Indicates how many days, on average, inventory is held before being sold.

Inventory Turnover Rate
Evaluates how efficiently inventory is cycled over a given period.

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of operational efficiency and inventory health.

📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following analytical views:

Warehouse Utilization Rate (Overview & Visual Trend)
High-level KPI tracking along with a visual representation to understand usage patterns.

Transportation Cost by Region and Category
Highlights cost concentration and helps identify expensive logistics segments.

Units Sold by Year
Shows sales trends over time to understand demand movement.

Lead Time by Product Category
Identifies categories with longer fulfillment delays.

Backorders by Order Status
Helps monitor fulfillment issues and operational bottlenecks.

Inventory Level by Category and Region
Provides a detailed breakdown of stock distribution to support inventory balancing decisions.

🔍 Insights Enabled by This Dashboard

Detect over- or under-utilized warehouses

Identify slow-moving vs fast-moving inventory

Spot regions or categories driving high transportation costs

Understand lead-time variability across product lines

Monitor backorder patterns affecting customer fulfillment
